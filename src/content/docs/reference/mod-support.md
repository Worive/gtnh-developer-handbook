---

Use the official
[mod support spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1LHd8c4FLLHiJqsuTppF2XFTE6bh-mXE7afVwGbzOKms)
and read its current legend. A repository living in the GTNewHorizons
organization does not by itself define its support status.

If you cannot find a repository link, GTNH may consume the upstream mod without
GTNH-specific changes. Confirm the mod identity first, then ask in the official
Discord's developer channels before creating a new fork or duplicate pull
request.
