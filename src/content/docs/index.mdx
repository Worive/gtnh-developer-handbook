> **This is an unofficial, community-maintained draft for GTNH developers. It is
> not endorsed by the GTNH team. Instructions may be incomplete or outdated;
> verify important details against the relevant repository.**

This handbook is for people changing GTNH repositories: mod developers, build
maintainers, reviewers, and first-time contributors. It is not player
documentation and does not replace the
[official GTNH wiki](https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/).

The content is an early draft. Each technical page points back to current
repository files or upstream documentation so you can verify anything that may
have changed.
